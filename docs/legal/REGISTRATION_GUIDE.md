# Music Industry Registration Guide

This guide walks you through FREE registrations needed BEFORE launching Strezless.

## 1. MLC (Mechanical Licensing Collective) - FREE

**What**: Required for paying mechanical royalties to songwriters  
**Cost**: FREE for digital service providers  
**Website**: https://www.themlc.com

### Steps:
1. Go to https://www.themlc.com/services/digital-service-providers
2. Click "Register as a DSP"
3. Provide:
   - Business name: Strezless
   - EIN: 86-1986359
   - Contact info
   - Service description
4. Sign the blanket license agreement
5. Set up monthly reporting system

**Timeline**: 2-4 weeks approval

---

## 2. SoundExchange - FREE

**What**: Collects digital performance royalties  
**Cost**: FREE registration  
**Website**: https://www.soundexchange.com

### Steps:
1. Go to https://www.soundexchange.com/service-provider/
2. Click "Register Your Service"
3. Provide:
   - Service details
   - Payment information
   - Reporting capabilities
4. Agree to terms
5. Set up monthly reporting

**Timeline**: 1-2 weeks approval

---

## 3. US Copyright Office - ISRC Registrar (FREE)

**What**: Get ability to generate ISRC codes for tracks  
**Cost**: FREE  
**Website**: https://usisrc.org

### Steps:
1. Go to https://usisrc.org/get-codes
2. Apply as a registrant
3. Provide business information
4. Receive registrant code (format: US-XXX-YY)
5. Use this to generate ISRCs for all tracks

**Format**: US-XXX-YY-NNNNN
- US = Country
- XXX = Your registrant code
- YY = Year
- NNNNN = Unique track number

**Timeline**: Instant to 1 week

---

**Cost**: FREE  
**Website**: Through your local PRO or directly

## 6. DDEx Implementer (For Later) - $1,500+/year

**What**: Standard for delivering music metadata to DSPs  
**Cost**: $1,500-$3,000/year  
**Website**: https://ddex.net

### When to join:
- After you have 10+ artists
- When partnering with major DSPs directly
- Not needed initially if using aggregators

---

## 7. Google Play Developer - $25 one-time

**What**: Publish Android apps  
**Cost**: $25 one-time fee  
**Website**: https://play.google.com/console

### Steps:
1. Go to Google Play Console
2. Create developer account
3. Pay $25 fee
4. Verify identity
5. Set up merchant account (for paid apps/IAP)

---

## 8. Apple Developer Program - $99/year

**What**: Publish iOS apps  
**Cost**: $99/year  
**Website**: https://developer.apple.com/programs/

### Steps:
1. Go to Apple Developer
2. Enroll in program
3. Pay $99/year
4. Verify identity (D-U-N-S number needed for business)
5. Accept agreements

---

## 9. F-Droid Submission - FREE

**What**: Open-source Android app store  
**Cost**: FREE  
**Website**: https://f-droid.org

### Requirements:
- App must be 100% open source
- Reproducible builds
- No proprietary libraries
- Submit metadata via GitLab
