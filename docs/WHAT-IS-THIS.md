# What Is This Repository?

## The Simple Answer

This is a **music business documentation toolkit** created by Sin Estres (Omar Orrantia) for Strezless Musick Productionz.

If you're wondering "what's this?" - here's what you need to know:

### 🎯 Purpose
Help musicians and producers manage the business side of making music, especially when collaborating with others.

### 🎵 What Problem Does It Solve?

When you create music with other people, you need to answer:
- **Who owns what?** (ownership percentages)
- **Who wrote what?** (lyrics vs. melody vs. production)
- **How do we get paid?** (PRO registrations, publisher info)
- **What did we agree on?** (written documentation)

Without proper documentation, these questions can lead to disputes, lost money, and broken relationships.

### 📦 What's Inside?

This repository provides:

1. **Templates** - Ready-to-use forms you can fill out
   - Split sheets for tracking song ownership
   - Documentation for legal records
   - Collaboration agreements

2. **Guides** - How to use the templates
   - Quick start guide
   - Best practices
   - Industry standards

3. **Organization** - A system for managing music business docs
   - Consistent formatting
   - Clear structure
   - Easy to share with lawyers, PROs, publishers

### 👥 Who Is This For?

- **Music Producers** starting their own label/business
- **Singer/Songwriters** working with collaborators
- **Independent Artists** who want professional documentation
- **Music Business Students** learning industry practices
- **Anyone** who needs to track music rights and ownership

### 🚀 How Do I Use It?

1. Clone this repository
2. Go to `/docs/` folder
3. Find the template you need (e.g., split sheet)
4. Copy it and fill in your information
5. Store it securely
6. Share only with authorized parties (lawyers, PROs, publishers)

### ⚠️ What This Is NOT

- ❌ Not a music production software
- ❌ Not a recording tool
- ❌ Not a digital audio workstation (DAW)
- ❌ Not legal advice (always consult a lawyer for legal matters)

### 💡 Example Use Case

**Scenario**: You and 2 friends just wrote a song together.

**Problem**: How do you document who wrote what and how to split the money?

**Solution**: 
1. Use the split sheet template from `/docs/splits/`
2. Fill in everyone's information
3. Assign percentages (must add up to 100%)
4. Everyone signs it
5. Register with your PRO (ASCAP, BMI, etc.)
6. Now you have a legal record of ownership!
