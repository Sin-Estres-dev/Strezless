# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Strezless Musick Productionz! This guide will help you get started with using the repository.

## For New Users

### What is this repository?
This is a documentation and template repository for Strezless Musick Productionz. It helps you manage the business side of music production, including rights management, legal documentation, and collaboration.

### What can I do here?

1. **Use Templates**
   - Navigate to `/docs/splits/` for split sheet templates
   - Fill out templates for your music projects
   - Store completed documents securely

2. **Track Song Rights**
   - Document who wrote what parts of your songs
   - Track ownership percentages
   - Record PRO information for each writer
   - Maintain publisher details

3. **Collaborate**
   - Share templates with your team
   - Standardize how you document projects
   - Keep all parties informed and documented

## Using a Split Sheet Template

1. Navigate to `/docs/splits/split-sheet-5-writers.md`
2. Copy the template to your local workspace
3. Fill in:
   - Song information (title, date, ISWC/ISRC codes)
   - Writer details (names, PROs, roles)
   - Share percentages (must total 100%)
4. Collect signatures from all writers
5. Store the completed document securely

## Privacy & Security

⚠️ **Important**: Split sheets contain personal information:
- Store completed documents securely
- Limit access to authorized parties only
- Share only with necessary people (PROs, publishers, legal counsel)
- Follow data retention policies
